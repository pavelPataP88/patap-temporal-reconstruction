## Gate failure before external execution

The existing recorder learns read/write paths from `strace` and only calls
`digest(path)` after the build process has terminated. That cannot establish
the byte content of an artifact *when a particular operation consumed it*:
the path can be overwritten, replaced, removed, or renamed between the logged
open and post-build hashing. As a result, path-level events cannot be promoted
honestly to realized artifact-instance provenance.

This fails the mandatory fingerprint-timing gate. Consequently no claim is
made that any of the three projects built, produced valid traces, or yielded
valid evaluator truth under this preflight. The previous external workflow is
not rerun, no new scientific protocol freeze is created, and extractor E is
unchanged.

## Required next engineering work

A future preflight would need a recorder that captures immutable read content
at read time and completed output content at write-close/atomic publication,
including rename and subprocess semantics. It must be independently tested on
all three pinned projects before any further v0.5 scientific freeze.
